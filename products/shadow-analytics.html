Section 01 • Business Friction

The Problem: The Multi-Billion Dollar PDF Trap

Why manual document reading paralyzes finance, legal, and enterprise compliance operations.

🎯 The Target User

Enterprise procurement teams, accounts payable auditors, and compliance analysts ingesting 500 to 15,000 unstructured PDFs every month.

⚠️ Why OCR Breaks

Documents arrive in unpredictable formats—scanned images, rotated pages, multi-column tables. Legacy template-based OCR fails whenever layouts shift by even 5 pixels.

💸 The High Cost

A mid-sized team processing 2,500 documents at 30 minutes each burns 1,250 labor hours ($40,000/month) with a 4–8% typographical error rate.

Section 02 • Core Product Value

The Solution: End-to-End Auditable Intelligence

Transforming raw unstructured PDF streams into confidence-scored, ERP-ready tabular data.

⚡ 8-Second Extraction

Extracts key entities, nested invoice line items, and payment terms into clean JSON schemas in under 8 seconds.

📐 Bounding Box Verification

Clicking any extracted figure instantly highlights its exact pixel bounding box on the original PDF canvas for 100% auditability.

🔍 Exception-Only Workflow

Calibrated confidence thresholds flag low-confidence fields for human review, reducing manual effort by ~85% while maintaining 98%+ accuracy.

Section 03 • Strategic Execution

The Approach: Audited Schemas Over Chatbot Toys

Why predictable tabular schemas and verified provenance beat conversational chatbots.

🚫 Why Chatbots Fail

Enterprise operators don't want to chat with PDFs. Chatting requires prompt formulation on every page. Teams need predictable tabular columns.

🤖 Hybrid OCR + LLM Strategy

Paired Tesseract OCR coordinate tokenization with Google Gemini Multimodal LLMs under strict JSON schema constraints to eliminate hallucinations.

🛡️ Trade-offs Evaluated

Rejected brittle heuristic regex rules and costly fine-tuned model hosting in favor of zero-shot prompt engineering with spatial RAG indexing.

📐 Bounding Box Traceability

Clicking on any extracted field instantly pans and highlights the exact bounding box polygon on the document preview canvas.

🎛️ Custom Extraction Schemas

Users define extraction schemas visually—such as adding a field for "Net 30 Discount" or "Jurisdiction"—without writing code.

Section 05 • Input Telemetry & Ingestion

The Data Gathering: Corpus Ingestion & Validation

How enterprise documents are collected, spatialized, and evaluated against ground truth.

📥 Ingestion Channels

Direct browser drag-and-drop, automated email inbox attachments, and REST API endpoints receiving multi-page PDFs, TIFFs, and PNG scans.

🔬 Coordinate Normalization

Tokenizes text and bounding box geometries via Tesseract OCR, normalizing DPI differences and rotation angles into uniform polygons.

📊 Benchmark Evaluation

Tested on 220+ ground-truth enterprise documents (invoices, NDAs, bills of lading), verifying 90–98% numeric accuracy and <8s turnaround.

Section 06 • Scalability & Commercialization

The Future Business Scope: Autonomous Enterprise Workflows

Scaling from isolated document extraction into self-healing enterprise workflow automation.

Phase 01

3-Way Automated Reconciliation

Cross-reference extracted invoice totals against Purchase Orders and Goods Receipt Notes (GRN) to catch billing discrepancies before payment.

Phase 02

Direct ERP Synchronization

Direct two-way push to SAP, NetSuite, and QuickBooks ledgers via standard OAuth connectors, eliminating manual CSV export/import steps.

Phase 03

Self-Learning Accuracy Loops

Human bounding box corrections feed vector few-shot memory, boosting future extraction accuracy on recurring vendor layouts automatically.
