Section 01 • Business Friction

The Problem: The Clinical Adherence Cliff

Why 60% of patients drop out of post-consultation recovery protocols within 14 days.

🎯 The Target Patient

Outpatient recovery individuals, mental wellness seekers, and physical rehabilitation patients managing post-consultation regimens.

⚠️ The 30-Day Blindspot

Once patients leave the clinic with static paper handouts, therapists and doctors have zero insight into daily recovery or mood fluctuations.

💸 Preventable Relapse

Overwhelmed by intimidating clinical questionnaires, patients abandon habits within 14 days, leading to avoidable clinical relapses.

Section 02 • Core Product Value

The Solution: Micro-Checkins & Real-Time Audio Synthesis

Conversational daily triage, real-time synthesized audio soundscapes, and automated provider summaries.

🤖 60-Second Empathetic Check-ins

Low-friction conversational companion asking 2 simple questions daily to track mood, sleep, and exercise completion without homework fatigue.

🧘 Dynamic Audio Synthesis

Web Audio API generates continuous real-time soundscapes (Alpha 10Hz, Theta 6Hz) for stress regulation with zero MP3 file download lag.

📊 30-Second Clinician Digest

Compresses 30 days of patient telemetry into a concise visual overview for physicians right before monthly consultations begin.

Section 03 • Strategic Execution

The Approach: Low Cognitive Load & Client-Side Privacy

Why behavioral adherence requires conversational empathy and zero clinical jargon.

🚫 Zero Clinical Jargon

Intimidating medical surveys induce anxiety. Nirvana speaks like an empathetic friend while mapping responses to clinical metrics behind the scenes.

⚡ Browser Audio Generation

Synthesizing binaural sound waves directly in the browser via Web Audio API saves bandwidth and provides unlimited procedural variety.

🛡️ Strict HIPAA & Privacy

Enforced end-to-end client-side encryption and minimal data collection to preserve patient confidentiality and trust.

Section 05 • Input Telemetry & Ingestion

The Data Gathering: Daily Check-ins & Acoustic Telemetry

How patient inputs, biometric mood markers, and audio therapy session logs are captured.

📝 Daily Conversational Entries

Captures subjective mood rating (1–10), sleep quality indicators, protocol completion checkboxes, and unstructured free-text reflections.

🎧 Audio Session Telemetry

Tracks soundscape preset utilization (Alpha vs. Theta wave sessions), listening duration, pause events, and pre/post stress scores.

🔒 Encrypted Storage

All patient health telemetry is encrypted with AES-256 at rest, guaranteeing strict compliance with clinical data protection guidelines.

Section 06 • Scalability & Commercialization

The Future Business Scope: FHIR & EHR Interoperability

Integrating daily patient wellness directly into clinical medical records.

Phase 01

FHIR Standard Data Export

Structure check-in telemetry and pain scores into HL7 / FHIR JSON formats for seamless direct export into Epic and Cerner EHR records.

Phase 02

Wearable Sensor Sync

Sync heart rate variability (HRV) and sleep cycles from Apple Watch and Oura ring to correlate biometric recovery with mood check-ins.

Phase 03

Clinical Risk Alert Webhooks

Automated urgent webhooks dispatched directly to on-call duty nurses when severe post-operative symptoms are detected.
