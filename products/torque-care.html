Section 01 • Business Friction

The Problem: The Hidden Friction of Informal Vehicle Care

Why millions of urban vehicle owners overpay for unperformed services every month.

🎯 The Target User

Apartment car owners paying neighborhood cleaners on recurring informal monthly cash agreements ($20 to $50/month).

⚠️ The Unspoken Misses

Cleaners miss 4 to 8 unannounced days monthly due to leave, weather, or absenteeism. Without daily records, owners forget exact dates.

💸 Confrontational Settlement

End-of-month payments devolve into awkward disputes. Most car owners pay the full fee out of fatigue, losing 15–20% in unperformed services.

Section 02 • Core Product Value

The Solution: Daily Transparency & Automated Pro-Rata Math

Objective daily attendance logging, holiday exemptions, and itemized WhatsApp settlement receipts.

📅 One-Tap Daily Logger

A 2-second single-tap calendar interface marking vehicle status: Cleaned (green), Agreed Holiday (yellow), or Missed (red).

⚡ Automated Pro-Rata Formula

Mathematically divides agreed monthly rate by actual working days, charging strictly for delivered cleanings with zero rounding bias.

🧾 Respectful WhatsApp Receipt

Generates a polite, clear WhatsApp breakdown with payment link: "22 days cleaned, 4 Sundays off, 5 missed. Net payable: $22.00."

Section 03 • Strategic Execution

The Approach: Software Accountability, Not Service Delivery

Why building a software utility beats running an operations-heavy cleaning workforce.

🚫 Zero Service Provision Risk

Avoided operations-heavy cleaning models with high labor turnover and thin margins. Torque Care operates purely as an objective accounting layer.

📱 Zero-Hardware CapEx

Rejected fragile NFC tags or windshield Bluetooth beacons. A lightweight mobile web PWA provides immediate value with zero installation cost.

🛡️ Mutual Trust Protocol

Clear visual logs remove personal friction, protecting both owners from overpaying and honest cleaners from false wage deductions.

Section 05 • Input Telemetry & Ingestion

The Data Gathering: Vehicle Profiles & Service Telemetry

How vehicle records, cleaning frequencies, and daily attendance logs are stored.

🚗 Vehicle & Rate Contracts

Vehicle registration number, make/model category, assigned cleaner contact, agreed monthly lump-sum fee, and cleaning schedule rules.

📅 Attendance Status Telemetry

Timestamped daily Boolean markers recording Cleaned (True), Missed (False), or Excluded (Sunday/Public Holiday) stored in Firebase Firestore.

🧾 Settlement Transaction Logs

Monthly settlement snapshots logging total working days, served days, deducted amount, net amount transferred, and payment confirmation status.

Section 06 • Scalability & Commercialization

The Future Business Scope: Residential PropTech Integrations

Scaling from individual car owners to gated community facility management.

Phase 01

Multi-Vehicle Family Dashboard

Enable household members to share a single vehicle calendar so either spouse can tap and mark attendance without duplicate logging.

Phase 02

Gate Management ERP Sync

Provide automated CSV/JSON exports formatted for popular residential management ERPs (e.g. MyGate, ADDA, NoBrokerHood).

Phase 03

Cleaner Payout Ledger

A multilingual, audio-enabled cleaner portal where service staff can view their confirmed earnings across all 20+ flats they clean in a building.
