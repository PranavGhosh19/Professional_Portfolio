Section 01 • Business Friction

The Problem: Eliminating the 90-Minute Post-Round Wait

Why manual paper tournament scoring kills the excitement of competitive golf.

🎯 The Target User

Golf club committees, corporate tournament organizers, PGA teaching professionals, and amateur players competing in 50- to 150-player events.

⚠️ The Paper Card Bottleneck

Tournaments rely on paper cards collected at the 18th hole. Committee staff spend 1.5 to 2 hours deciphering messy handwriting and calculating handicaps manually.

💸 Lost Tournament Drama

Clubhouse spectators and golfers have zero idea who is leading until the evening banquet, killing the live competitive energy of the sport.

Section 02 • Core Product Value

The Solution: Live Mobile Scoring & Clubhouse TV Broadcasts

Real-time leaderboards, automated handicap rules, and instant post-round celebrations.

📱 Live Mobile Scoring

One designated marker per group records hole-by-hole strokes in a high-contrast interface built specifically for bright outdoor sunlight.

📺 4K Clubhouse TV Broadcast

Full-screen auto-scrolling leaderboard displaying live rankings, hole-in-one alerts, and sponsor banners on clubhouse television screens.

⚡ Instant Rule Calculus

Automated scoring engine handles Gross, Net, Modified Stableford, Scramble, and Peoria handicap systems with zero delay.

Section 03 • Strategic Execution

The Approach: Software Simplicity Over Hardware Overkill

Delivering 99% of live tournament value through accessible web apps without expensive sensor hardware.

🚫 Why Hardware Fails

On-course sensors and camera trackers cost thousands of dollars, suffer from battery drain, and slow down play. A mobile web app works on any phone instantly.

⚡ 5-Second Tee Box UX

Golfers want to focus on their swing. The interface requires just a single tap per player, completing group hole entry in under 5 seconds.

🛡️ Sub-300ms WebSockets

Used real-time WebSocket pub/sub broadcasting instead of HTTP polling, ensuring instant leaderboard updates when players sink their 18th putt.

⛳ Multi-Format Tournament Engine

Supports Individual Stroke Play, Better-Ball, Scramble, Modified Stableford, and System 36/Peoria handicap adjustments simultaneously.

📺 TV Broadcast Mode

A specialized widescreen layout designed for 4K clubhouse televisions, with auto-scrolling leaderboard rankings, sponsor branding, and hole-in-one alerts.

📈 Player Performance Cards

Post-round analytics displaying Greens in Regulation (GIR), Fairways Hit percentage, putting averages, and hole-by-hole scoring trends.

Section 05 • Input Telemetry & Ingestion

The Data Gathering: Course Telemetry & Player Scoring

How golf course slope parameters, hole-by-hole scores, and telemetry feeds are ingested.

⛳ Course Slope & Rating Feed

Ingests USGA/WHS course ratings, tee box yardages, hole pars, and stroke index difficulty allocations across all 18 holes.

📱 On-Course Live Scores

Captures gross strokes, putts, penalty counts, and fairway accuracy indicators logged hole-by-hole from players' mobile devices.

🎯 Simulator Telemetry Integration

Ingests launch monitor telemetry feeds (ball velocity, launch angle, clubhead speed, spin axis) for indoor simulator competitions.

Section 06 • Scalability & Commercialization

The Future Business Scope: Multi-Round League Operations

Expanding from single-day events to season-long corporate golf leagues.

Phase 01

Sponsor Overlay Ad Engine

Allow corporate sponsors (auto brands, private banks) to rotate dynamic video and banner ads across clubhouse screens.

Phase 02

GPS Green Distance Overlay

Integrate satellite golf course polygons to display Front/Center/Back green yardages directly on the mobile scorecard interface.

Phase 03

Season-Long League Standings

FedEx Cup-style season point tracking for recurring weekend groups and corporate golf societies over 12 to 24 fixtures.
