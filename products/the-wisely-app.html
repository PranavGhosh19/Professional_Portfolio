Section 01 • Business Friction

The Problem: The Great Financial App Fragmentation

Why expense-splitting tools and budgeting apps leave users blind to their true net position.

🎯 The Target User

Young professionals, roommates, travel groups, and couples who manage shared living expenses, group weekend trips, and individual daily budgets simultaneously across multiple banking cards and apps.

⚠️ The Underlying Pain Point

Legacy tools like Splitwise operate strictly as peer-to-peer settlement ledgers. They tell you who owes whom, but know nothing about your monthly rent budget, credit card liabilities, or grocery targets. Conversely, budgeting apps treat group splits as random, distortive cash leaks.

💸 Reconciliation Friction

Friends end up with confusing circular debts, spending 25+ minutes cross-checking spreadsheets after every trip, while fronting dinner expenses causes cashflow distortion.

Section 02 • Core Product Value

The Solution: Unified Personal & Group Financial Intelligence

Bridging social spending and individual wealth into one seamless, real-time platform.

💳 Unified Net Position

A single dashboard showing your actual financial standing—combining personal bank balances with group liabilities and receivables.

⚡ Greedy Debt Minimizer

Graph-based flow network algorithms automatically consolidate complex group debts into the fewest direct transactions possible.

🤖 AI Spending Copilot

Plain-English conversational insights: "You fronted 72% of group dining expenses this month. You are owed $340 across 3 friends."

Section 03 • Strategic Execution

The Approach: Frictionless Onboarding & Mathematical Simplicity

Strategic product design choices that eliminate app-store barriers and simplify settlements.

📱 Zero-Install Web PWA

Bypassed app store download friction completely with a progressive web app that loads in <10 seconds via simple QR code scans.

🛡️ Mathematical Integrity

Replaced manual ledger math with a provably correct debt simplification algorithm guaranteeing all group accounts sum to zero.

🤝 Fair Monetization

Free forever for core group splits without intrusive paywalls, monetizing advanced AI insights and automated financial planning tools.

Section 05 • Input Telemetry & Ingestion

The Data Gathering: User Logs & Group Ledgers

How individual expenses and group split transactions are gathered, validated, and normalized.

📝 User Ingestion Inputs

Quick-entry expense modal collecting amount, currency, custom category tags (Rent, Dining, Flights), payer identity, and split weights.

🧾 Receipt Image Processing

Client-side image compression and OCR extraction parse total amount, tax breakdowns, and vendor names from camera snaps.

📊 Multi-Currency Normalization

Live FX rate feeds normalize international travel expenses into a single unified settlement currency for transparent group reconciliation.

Section 06 • Scalability & Commercialization

The Future Business Scope: Autonomous Household Banking

Scaling from split ledgers to automated predictive banking integrations.

Phase 01

Account Aggregator & Open Banking

Integrate Open Banking APIs (Plaid / Setu) for auto-fetching card transactions, eliminating manual entry for utility and grocery expenses.

Phase 02

Predictive Cashflow Warnings

Analyze historical recurring debits to warn roommates 7 days before rent is due if their personal checking balance is running low.

Phase 03

In-App UPI & Venmo Settlement

Deep-link one-tap settlement payments directly through UPI and payment gateways with instant automatic mark-as-paid receipt generation.
